BorderFlowAI 

The vision for BorderFlow AI is to build an "Agentic" solution that tackles the massive congestion and fraud at South African border posts, such as Beitbridge.

The Core Problem
The project aims to solve the 12-plus hour delays caused by outdated manual processing and the high prevalence of documentation fraud. Currently, these inefficiencies hinder regional trade and compromise national security.

The "Agentic" Solution
Rather than a simple dashboard, you envision an AI Agent that handles pre-clearance and forensic verification before a truck even reaches the border:

AI Verification: Using Gemini 3 Flash to scan complex documents like the Bill of Lading and driver IDs to detect anomalies or signs of tampering.

Immutable Trust Layer: Recording a "Proof of Cargo" hash on the Solana blockchain to ensure that once data is verified, it cannot be altered by third parties.

Real-Time Interaction: Utilizing ElevenLabs to provide automated voice alerts and status updates to drivers, streamlining the communication between officials and logistics teams.

The Ultimate Goal
The vision is to transform the border into a high-speed, secure gateway. By moving the "thinking" and "verifying" to an AI agent, you aim to reduce transit times significantly while simultaneously lowering the rates of illegal cargo and document forgery.
